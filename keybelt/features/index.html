Features

What KeyBelt does

Six tools for keys and animation, and the interface they live in. All of it is in the public beta. Each name links to its page in the documentation.

Tools

  • Keys Editor

    Sliders that act on the keys you have selected: smooth, push / pull, interpolate, ease, wave, offset and hierarchy offset. The curves change while you drag. Double-click a slider to reset it. Buttons next to the sliders delete flat or redundant keys and bake.

  • Baker

    Bakes animation to a key on every frame. Choose world space, the space of another object, or a camera's space. The list of baked controls stays, so you can bake the same set again.

  • Offseter

    Move or rotate the selected object and the animation follows, offset by the transform you gave it.

  • Reset All

    Sets every keyable channel of the selection back to its default. One undo step. Locked channels are skipped.

  • Selector

    A small always-on-top HUD for selection sets. Click a colour chip to select that set. Click a colour dot to filter.

  • Motion Trail

    Draws an object's path in the viewport, in world space or relative to the camera, with handles you can edit. Motion Trail started from the Agora Community motion trail and has been reworked and extended for KeyBelt.

Interface

  • Timeline toolbar

    The Keys Editor sliders and action buttons sit under Maya's Time Slider. Reorder or hide tools in Settings.

  • Graph Editor toolbar

    The same sliders and buttons, attached to the Graph Editor. If the editor is narrow, the toolbar is cut off at the right edge. It does not force the editor wider.

  • Radial menu

    Press Alt+Q for a wheel at the cursor. Over the viewport you get one wheel, over the Graph Editor another. You pick the tools for each in Settings.

  • Viewport bar

    A floating bar with your favourite tools, inside the viewport. It stays on screen while Maya is in the background.

  • Time Dragger HUD

    Hold the Time Dragger key (K by default) and drag with LMB or MMB to scrub with no screen-edge limit. A temporary timeline shows the playback range, the keys of your selection and bookmarks. Shift is fine, Ctrl is coarse, the wheel moves one frame, Esc returns to the start frame.

  • Settings

    One window for toolbars, hotkeys, the radial menu and viewport bar contents, startup and updates. Move and resize KeyBelt's floating windows with MMB / RMB drag.

What is not here yet

KeyBelt has no macOS or Linux build, and no support for Maya before 2023. More tools exist in my private toolset (Key Blender, Linker, Temporal IK and others). They are not part of KeyBelt today. See the list of upcoming tools.
