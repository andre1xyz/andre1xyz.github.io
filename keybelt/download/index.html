Install

  1. Download the zip.
  2. Extract it anywhere. Keep the KeyBelt folder together.
  3. Drag KeyBelt/drag_n_drop.py into the Maya viewport. KeyBelt installs itself, adds a KeyBelt shelf tab and starts with Maya from then on.

Updates

KeyBelt checks for new versions in the background. To update, open Settings > General > Updates and click Download update. Nothing is downloaded before you click. The update is applied the next time Maya starts, and your settings are kept.
